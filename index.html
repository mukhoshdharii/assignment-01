Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you register.

What's included in my ticket?

Every ticket tier includes access to all keynote and track talks. Pro and Team tiers add workshop access, recordings, and networking events — see the pricing section above for the full breakdown by tier.

Is there a virtual attendance option?

DevConf 2026 is an in-person event only. Standard and Pro ticket holders get digital recordings of all sessions after the conference so you won't miss a talk even if you can't make every session live.

What's your refund policy?

Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. Within 30 days, tickets can be transferred to a colleague at no charge, but are non-refundable.

Can I upgrade my ticket after registering?

Yes. You can upgrade from Standard to Pro at any time before the event by paying the price difference. Reach out to our team to upgrade to a Team ticket.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, all attendees, speakers, and sponsors are expected to follow our code of conduct, which covers respectful behavior, harassment policy, and how to report concerns on-site.

Do you offer student or group discounts?

Students get 50% off Standard tickets with a valid .edu email. Groups of 5 or more from the same company should use the Team tier or contact us for custom pricing.
